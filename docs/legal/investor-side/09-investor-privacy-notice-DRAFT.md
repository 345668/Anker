# Privacy notice for investors: DRAFT

*Draft Article 13 notice for people who sign up as investors. **Not published. Not legal advice.** For counsel to correct, complete and approve; bracketed items are facts the founder must supply. Replace the general `/privacy` link in the sign-up consent with this notice once approved (change the wording in `lib/investor/legal.ts`).*

**Who we are.** [Company name, address, email, data-protection contact]. We run Anker, a platform for venture funds, founders and investors.

**What this notice covers.** Your investor account on Anker: finding funds, asking funds to talk, conversations with funds, your investor documents, and being found by funds if you choose.

**What we collect and why.**
- *Your account:* email address, name, password (stored by our sign-in provider as a hash), the time you accepted the terms. To give you an account (contract).
- *Your profile and entities:* name, country, languages, how you want to be contacted, a short description; for each entity you invest through (yourself, a company, a trust, a family office): its name, kind, country, tax residence, the category of investor you say you are, and what you look for (sectors, stages, regions, cheque size, time horizon). To show you funds that fit and to let you ask them to talk (contract; steps at your request).
- *Your requests and conversations:* the requests you send, what you chose to share, the messages and files you exchange with a fund, NDAs you accept (a copy of the text, the time, your IP address and a hash of your browser details, as evidence), meetings you arrange. To carry out what you asked for (steps at your request); the NDA record to keep evidence (legitimate interest).
- *Your investor documents (optional):* copies of identity and address documents you add to your passport, and a record of which fund received a copy and when. Only if you add them, and only shared with a fund when you choose (consent).
- *Being found (optional, off by default):* if you switch it on for an entity, funds that fit see a card without your name, email or team. We record when you switched it on (consent).
- *Security and abuse prevention:* sign-up attempts and limits, an activity log of who did what on your entity (legitimate interest).

**Who receives it.** A fund only receives what you choose to share when you ask to talk or accept its invitation, and your email address when it accepts; documents only when you share them. Our service providers process data for us: [list from `/privacy/subprocessors`: hosting, database, sign-in, email delivery]. We do not send your investor data to AI providers.

**Where.** [Regions; transfers outside the EU and the safeguards, once confirmed.]

**How long.** See the table [generated from `08`]. In short: pending codes a day; your account and data until you delete them; funds you have dealt with keep their own records, with your name removed if you erase your account; files in a closed conversation a year; passport files 30 days after you archive the entity.

**Your rights.** Access and a copy (Download my data), correction (edit your profile), erasure (Delete my investor account), withdrawal of consent (switch off, take back a shared document), restriction and objection (email [address]), and complaint to your supervisory authority [name].

**Funds are separate controllers.** Once you ask a fund to talk, the fund also holds the information you gave it and is responsible for it under its own privacy notice. Ask the fund to correct or delete what it holds.

**Automated decisions.** We rank funds by how well their stated focus matches what you say you look for. This is not a decision with legal or similar effect and is not advice.
