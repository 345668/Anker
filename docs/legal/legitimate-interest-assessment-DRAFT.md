# Legitimate-interest assessment: the investor directory (DRAFT)

*Draft for review. Not legal advice. Bracketed items need the founder's facts or counsel.*

**1. Purpose.** Help founders find, and be introduced to, investors whose published focus fits their company; help investors
receive relevant introductions. A genuine, lawful, specific interest of Anker and of its customers.

**2. Necessity.** The matching cannot be done without a record of who the investor is, the firm, and the stated focus. We hold
the minimum professional fields (§ notice: what we hold) and exclude personal contact details and special-category data.
[Alternatives considered: matching on firm-level data only. Rejected for person-level outreach because introductions go to
people; firm-only data is used where no person is needed.]

**3. Balancing.**

| Factor | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Nature of the data | Professional, largely published; low sensitivity |
| Reasonable expectations | Investors expect inbound pitches in their professional role; they do not expect to be bought and sold as a list. Mitigation: notice at first contact, easy objection |
| Impact on the person | Low: a possible relevant email or message, which they can stop with one click |
| Vulnerable people | None expected; the directory is professional investors |
| Safeguards | Notice at first contact; one-click unsubscribe and global suppression; no sale of data; per-source licence; retention limit; erasure tooling; send gates by country; human approval before any send |

**4. Conclusion.** [Legitimate interest is available for the directory, subject to the safeguards above and counsel's
confirmation, in particular for EU recipients and for any email contact in Germany.]

**5. Review.** Re-assess each year, on a regulator query, or when sources, purposes or recipients change.
