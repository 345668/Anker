# Counsel brief: six questions for a fixed-fee review

*Send at the counsel trigger (docs/architecture/37 §8.7): before the first paid customer, publishing the directory notice,
enabling cold email to any EU country, or announcing the LP product.*

**About Anker.** A venture platform operated from the EU. Customers are founders, venture fund managers and their LPs. It
holds a directory of about 21,000 firms and 50,000 investor records from public sources, imports and licensed lists; it lets a
customer run approval-gated email and LinkedIn outreach to those people; AI features send customer documents to model
providers (Alibaba Cloud in Singapore by default, OpenAI, Mistral).

1. **Directory.** Lawful basis, Article 14 approach and retention for the person records. Drafts attached: privacy notice,
   legitimate-interest assessment.
2. **Cold email.** UWG section 7(2) no. 2 requires prior express consent for email advertising and has no business exemption.
   Is a founder's fundraising email to an investor "advertising"? What consent or relationship evidence is needed, by country
   (Germany first, then other EU states)? Current default: no cold email to German recipients without the customer's attestation.
3. **Roles and liability.** Platform versus customer responsibility for outreach; processor or controller for the contact
   data customers upload; the customer data-processing agreement; terms wording.
4. **LinkedIn.** The extension reads connections and automates messages in the customer's own session; the user agreement
   (section 8.2) prohibits bots, browser add-ons that scrape, and automated messaging. Exposure to Anker and to customers; wording.
5. **Transfers and providers.** Alibaba Cloud (Singapore) and other non-EU providers: DPAs, transfer assessments, what to
   promise customers about training and retention.
6. **Regulatory scope.** Whether capital-call drafting, KYC triage and valuation tools place Anker inside a regulated
   activity, and the disclaimers needed.
