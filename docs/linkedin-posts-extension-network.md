## Post 1 — The overlooked warm intro (lead with the pain)
**Visual: graph shot**

The intro you need probably already exists. You just can't see it.

Every VC I know keeps a mental list of "people I should get introduced to."
And almost every one of them has a mutual connection they've forgotten about —
a former colleague, a co-investor from two funds ago, that operator they met at a conference in 2023.

The problem isn't your network. It's that your network is a list, not a map.

So we built the map.

One click syncs your LinkedIn connections into Anker. The graph shows every person, every mutual, every path — and when you look up someone new, it answers the only question that matters:

"Who can introduce me?"

This is my actual network: 1,500 people. The intro paths were there all along. I just couldn't see them.

## Post 2 — The 10,000-connection graveyard
**Visual: graph shot**

Your LinkedIn network is where relationships go to be forgotten.

10 years of connecting. Thousands of accepted requests.
And when you actually need someone — a fintech operator in Berlin, an LP who backs emerging managers — you scroll, you search, you give up, you send a cold email instead.

Cold outreach to someone two handshakes away is a self-inflicted wound.

We built the Anker extension to fix this:

→ Sync your entire connections list in one run (it handles thousands, in batches)
→ Every person lands in a live relationship graph — searchable by name, company, title
→ Captured profiles keep their headline, role and location — and flag when someone changes jobs
→ Warm-intro paths surface automatically from your mutual connections

Your network stops being an archive and starts being infrastructure.

Live on the Chrome Web Store. Link in comments.

## Post 3 — Job changes are the trigger everyone misses
**Visual: overlay shot**

The best time to reconnect with someone is the week they change jobs.

New role, new mandate, new budget, new problems — and genuinely delighted to hear from people they already know.

Almost nobody catches these moments, because nobody re-reads their connections list.

Anker does it automatically: re-sync your network and every captured profile is diffed against the last capture. Different company? Different title? Flagged as a mover — in your graph, in your CRM, right on their LinkedIn profile when you visit it.

A relationship you invested in three years ago becomes relevant again — and you're the first to notice.

Chrome extension is live — link in the comments.
