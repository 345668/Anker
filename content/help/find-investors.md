Find investors ranks investors against your company profile. Each result has a score from 0 to 100, built from six factors: investor type, cheque size, sector, geography, thesis and whether a contact is known.

- **{{tier:prospect_c}}**: {{tier:prospect_c.min}} or above

## Using it
Open a result to see why it ranked where it did, then save it to your [pipeline](/dashboard/help/pipeline).

## What it cannot do
A score is a ranking aid, not a prediction that an investor will invest. Where a fact is unknown the score says so rather than guessing. Complete your company profile first, because the ranking can only be as good as what it knows about you.
