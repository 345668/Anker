Anker is a workspace for raising and managing capital. What you see depends on your role: founders get fundraising tools, funds get deal and investor tools, and limited partners get a read-only view of their own holdings.

## How the menu is organised
The menu groups pages by the work you do, not by feature name. Founders see areas such as Fundraising, Relationships, Outreach, Company, Documents and Assistant. Funds see Deals, Portfolio, Investors, Fund operations, Reporting and Assistant. Limited partners see Assistant, Capital activity and Documents.

## Where to start
1. Open the **Assistant** and ask a question about your own data.
2. Use the **command palette** (Ctrl K, or Cmd K on a Mac) to jump to any page by name.
3. Read [Workspaces, roles and switching](/dashboard/help/workspaces-and-roles) so you know whose data you are looking at.

## What it cannot do
Anker does not move money, and it does not send messages or change records without you approving them. Some pages only appear when your workspace's plan includes them.
