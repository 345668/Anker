The assistant answers questions using the data in the workspace you are in: your pipeline, contacts, calls and documents.

## Reading and proposing
Reading is immediate. When an answer would **change** something, such as moving an investor to a new stage or creating a follow-up task, the assistant does not do it. It adds a proposal to the [actions inbox](/dashboard/help/actions-inbox), where you approve or reject it.

## Good questions
- Which investors have not replied in two weeks?
- Summarise my last call with this investor.
- What is missing from my data room?

## What it cannot do
The assistant can be wrong. Check names, numbers and dates before you rely on them. It cannot send email or messages by itself and it does not see other workspaces. Use of it counts against your workspace's monthly allowance on the **Usage** page.
