Press **Ctrl K** (Windows, Linux) or **Cmd K** (Mac) anywhere in the app to open the command palette. Type part of a page name and press Enter.

## What it cannot do
It lists pages your role and plan can open. A page that is missing from it is not available to your workspace; see [Workspaces, roles and switching](/dashboard/help/workspaces-and-roles).
