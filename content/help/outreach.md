Outreach drafts messages for investors in your pipeline. You review each draft before anything is sent.

## The flow
1. Pick investors and a campaign.
2. Review the drafts and edit them.
3. Approve. Only approved messages are sent.

## Limits
Your plan sets a daily sending limit. When you reach it, further messages wait for the next day.

## What it cannot do
Whether a message reaches an inbox, and whether anyone replies, is outside Anker's control.
