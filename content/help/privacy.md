Your workspace's records are visible to the people you invite to it. They are kept apart from every other workspace.

## Platform operators
The people who run Anker can see how the platform is used, such as counts and errors. They are firewalled from the private records of a workspace.

## The assistant
The assistant reads your workspace data to answer you. Your records are not shared with other workspaces.

## What it cannot do
This page is a summary, not a contract. Your agreement and privacy policy are the binding terms.
