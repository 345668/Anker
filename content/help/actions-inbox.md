The actions inbox lists proposals made by the assistant and by automated helpers. Each shows what it would change and why.

## Deciding
For each proposal you can **approve**, **reject**, or, after approving, **undo** where the change can be reversed. Nothing happens until you approve.

## What it cannot do
Some changes cannot be undone, such as a message that has already been sent. Those are labelled before you approve.
