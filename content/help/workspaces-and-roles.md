A **workspace** is one organisation's space: its profile, contacts, documents and history. Everything you create belongs to the workspace, not to you personally, so a colleague you invite sees the same records.

## Roles
Your role in a workspace sets the menu you see. A person can belong to more than one workspace and switch between them. When you switch, the menu, the assistant and every number on screen change to that workspace.

## Inviting people
Open **Settings** to invite a colleague. The number of seats is set by your workspace's plan.

## What it cannot do
Switching workspace does not copy data between them. A workspace owner's private records are not visible to the platform's operators.
